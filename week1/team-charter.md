# Team Charter

## GreenLeaf Logistics – Beat-Bot Team

## 1. Team Name

**Team Name:** Releaf

---

## 2. Team Mission

To design and deliver a reliable and secure AI assistant that answers employee questions using company rules, reduces repetitive work, and avoids incorrect or unsafe answers.

---

## 3. Team Purpose

The purpose of this team is to:

- Turn business needs into a working AI assistant
- Build a system that follows company rules and can be trusted
- Help reduce repetitive questions for admins
- Deliver a high-quality product on time
- Create a scalable and maintainable technical foundation

---

## 4. Team Roles & Responsibilities

### Product Owner (PO)

**Responsibilities:**

- Define product vision and priorities
- Own backlog and user story refinement
- Ensure alignment with stakeholder expectations
- Accept or reject completed work
- Define success criteria

---

### Scrum Master

**Responsibilities:**

- Facilitate Agile ceremonies (standups, planning, retrospectives)
- Remove blockers for the team
- Ensure Agile practices are followed
- Support team communication and collaboration

---

### Technical Architect

**Responsibilities:**

- Define system architecture and technical decisions
- Ensure scalability, security, and maintainability
- Review technical implementations
- Guide integration of AI, backend, and frontend components

---

### Developers (Frontend & Backend)

**Responsibilities:**

- Implement features according to requirements
- Collaborate on API and system integration
- Write clean, maintainable, and testable code
- Participate in code reviews

---

### AI Engineer

**Responsibilities:**

- Design and implement AI components (retrieval, prompting, response logic)
- Ensure answers are accurate and follow company rules
- Prevent hallucinations and handle safe refusals
- Work closely with developers to integrate AI into the system
- Improve model performance through testing and iteration

---
### QA / Quality Engineer

**Responsibilities:**

- Define test strategy and acceptance validation
- Validate functionality against requirements
- Ensure accuracy, reliability, and edge-case handling
- Support evaluation of AI responses

---

## 6. Ground Rules - Working Agreements

## Meetings
- Keep standups short and to the point  
- Be present in the meetings 
- Be on time 

## Team Culture
- Be respectful  
- Give honest and helpful feedback  
- Support each other

### Communication and Raising Issues

- Daily standups for our transparency
- Blockers must be communicated soon
- Let's not assume, ask if something is unclear  

---

### Decision Making

- Try to agree as a team  
- If we can’t decide quickly, the Product Owner (or lead) decides  
- Focus on what’s best for the user and the project
- Disagreements: resolved through discussion and open communication

---

### Collaboration

- Code reviews required for all major changes
- Pair programming encouraged for complex tasks
- No work is considered complete without team visibility

---

## 7. Issue Escalation Process

- Identify the issue (technical, product, or team-related)
- Raise it in standup or team channel
- Escalate to Scrum Master or Product Owner if unresolved
- Document outcome for future reference

---

## 8. Definition of Done (DoD)

A user story is considered **Done** when:

- Functionality is implemented and works as expected
- Acceptance criteria are fully met
- Code is reviewed and approved
- No critical bugs remain
- Responses are accurate and aligned with policy
- Source references are correctly included (if applicable)
- Security constraints are respected
- Feature is tested and validated

---

## 9. Definition of Ready (DoR)

A user story is considered **Ready** when:

- Clearly written in the format:
  - As a [role], I want [goal], so that [benefit]
- Acceptance criteria are defined
- Scope is understood by the team
- Dependencies are identified
- Story is estimated

---

---

## 11. Sprint Overview

- Sprint length: 1 week
- Daily standup: 15 minutes
- Sprint planning: at the start of each sprint
- Sprint review/demo: end of sprint
- Retrospective: after each sprint

---

## 12. Team Success Criteria

The team is successful if:

- Sprint goals are consistently met
- Deliverables are completed on time
- The product meets stakeholder expectations
- The team collaborates effectively
- Risks and blockers are managed proactively

---

## 13. Commitment Statement

As a team, we promise to work together to build a reliable and helpful AI assistant. We will follow Agile practices, stay focused on accuracy and security, and make sure users can trust what the bot tells them.
