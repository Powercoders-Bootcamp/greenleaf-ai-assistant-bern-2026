# Boundary Definition (Out of Scope)

## Purpose

This document defines what is intentionally excluded from the BeatBot system.

The goal is to ensure clarity, reduce scope creep, and maintain system reliability and safety.

All decisions are based on the defined MoSCoW scope and project priorities.

---

## Out of Scope

Based on the current MoSCoW prioritization, the following areas are explicitly excluded:

### HR and Administrative Operations

- Salary and compensation processing  
- Full HR case management (conflicts, complaints, legal advice)  

---

### Logistics and Operations

- Logistics tracking and delivery status  
- Operational workflows outside internal policies  
 

---

### System Scope Limitations

- External system integrations  
- Acting as a general-purpose chatbot  
- Providing answers not grounded in official company documents  

---

## Behavioral Boundaries

The system must:

- Only respond based on approved sources (handbook, policy documents)  
- Refuse to answer when information is missing or unclear  
- Avoid generating answers without verifiable sources  

---

## Alignment with MoSCoW Scope

These boundaries directly support the defined priorities:

- Ensuring accuracy over feature expansion  
- Preventing unsafe or non-compliant responses  
- Maintaining a focused MVP scope
