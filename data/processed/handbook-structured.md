## 1. Company Background

### History
GreenLeaf Logistics was founded in 1974 by Hans Egli in Basel. The company started with a single truck and grew steadily:

- 1985: 10 trucks
- 1998: First digital warehouse system in the region
- Today: International logistics provider combining Swiss precision with global reach

### Philosophy
- "A clean truck is a happy truck"
- Focus on trust in logistics operations
