# Handbook Key Rules

## Vacation
- Full-time employees get 25 paid vacation days per calendar year.
- Employees over 50 get 5 additional days.
- Vacation requests must be submitted via HR portal at least 3 weeks in advance.

## Holidays
- GreenLeaf observes Swiss national holidays.
- Basel-Stadt staff also observe Labor Day (May 1st) as a full holiday.
- Staff in other regions must check their local cantonal calendar.

## Bereavement Leave
- 3 paid days for spouse, child, or parent.
- 1 paid day for grandparents or siblings.
- More than 3 days requires CEO approval.

## IT / Security
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
- Internal Wi-Fi access requires staff device registration via MAC address with IT.
- Guest Wi-Fi password exists, but internal bot should not share sensitive internal data freely.

## Expenses
- Client lunches are reimbursable only if at least one external client is present.
- Maximum 35 CHF per person.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable.
- Receipts must be scanned using the ScanPro app.

## Sensitive Topics
- Harassment, bullying, and whistleblowing must not be handled by the bot.
- Users should contact the confidential ombudsman.
