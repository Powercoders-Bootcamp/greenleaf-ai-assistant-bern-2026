# 01. Project Overview

## Background
GreenLeaf Logistics needs an internal AI assistant that reduces repetitive employee questions and provides consistent answers based on official company documents.

## Main Objective
Deliver a safe, useful, and verifiable assistant that can answer common internal questions without inventing information.

## Stakeholder
Beat Müller, Head of Operations & HR, wants a system that reduces interruptions and gives reliable answers, especially for expense and holiday questions.

## Main Users
- Employees
- HR / Operations
- Internal support staff

## Main Use Cases
- Is a lunch reimbursable?
- Is May 1st a holiday in Basel?
- How many vacation days do full-time employees get?
- What is the process for leave requests?
- What topics should not be handled by the bot?

## Product Principle
If the assistant is not sure, it should not guess. It should clearly say that the answer is not available in the approved source.
