# Team Charter

## GreenLeaf Logistics – Beat-Bot Team

## 1. Team Name

**Team Name:** PolicyAI Squad

---

## 2. Team Mission

To design and deliver a reliable, secure, and source-driven AI assistant that automates repetitive employee questions while strictly adhering to company policies and ensuring zero tolerance for incorrect or unsafe responses.

---

## 3. Team Purpose

The purpose of this team is to:

- Translate business needs into a working AI product
- Build a trustworthy assistant grounded in company policy
- Reduce operational workload for Admin users
- Deliver a high-quality MVP within the defined project timeline
- Establish a scalable and maintainable technical foundation

---

## 4. Team Roles & Responsibilities

### Product Owner (PO)

**Responsibilities:**

- Define product vision and priorities
- Own backlog and user story refinement
- Ensure alignment with stakeholder expectations
- Accept or reject completed work
- Define success criteria

---

### Scrum Master

**Responsibilities:**

- Facilitate Agile ceremonies (standups, planning, retrospectives)
- Remove blockers for the team
- Ensure Agile practices are followed
- Support team communication and collaboration

---

### Technical Architect

**Responsibilities:**

- Define system architecture and technical decisions
- Ensure scalability, security, and maintainability
- Review technical implementations
- Guide integration of AI, backend, and frontend components

---

### Developers (Frontend & Backend)

**Responsibilities:**

- Implement features according to requirements
- Collaborate on API and system integration
- Write clean, maintainable, and testable code
- Participate in code reviews

---

### QA / Quality Engineer

**Responsibilities:**

- Define test strategy and acceptance validation
- Validate functionality against requirements
- Ensure accuracy, reliability, and edge-case handling
- Support evaluation of AI responses

---

## 5. Team Values & Principles

The team commits to the following values:

### 1. Accuracy over speed

We prioritize correct and reliable outputs over fast but incorrect solutions.

### 2. Accountability

Each team member owns their work and contributes to overall team success.

### 3. Transparency

We communicate openly about progress, blockers, and risks.

### 4. Simplicity

We aim for simple, effective solutions, especially in early stages.

### 5. Continuous Improvement

We learn from each sprint and continuously refine our process and product.

### 6. User Trust First

We design every feature to increase trust in the system.

---

## 6. Working Agreements (Ground Rules)

### Communication

- Daily standups are mandatory
- Blockers must be communicated immediately
- Key decisions are documented

---

### Decision Making

- Product decisions: Product Owner has final say
- Technical decisions: Architect leads, team collaborates
- Disagreements: resolved through discussion, escalated if needed

---

### Collaboration

- Code reviews required for all major changes
- Pair programming encouraged for complex tasks
- No work is considered complete without team visibility

---

### Availability

- Team members are expected to be available during agreed working hours
- Absences must be communicated in advance

---

## 7. Issue Escalation Process

1. Identify the issue (technical, product, or team-related)
2. Raise it in standup or team channel
3. Attempt team-level resolution
4. Escalate to Scrum Master or Product Owner if unresolved
5. Document outcome for future reference

---

## 8. Definition of Done (DoD)

A user story is considered **Done** when:

- Functionality is implemented and works as expected
- Acceptance criteria are fully met
- Code is reviewed and approved
- No critical bugs remain
- Responses are accurate and aligned with policy
- Source references are correctly included (if applicable)
- Security constraints are respected
- Feature is tested and validated
- Documentation is updated if needed

---

## 9. Definition of Ready (DoR)

A user story is considered **Ready** when:

- Clearly written in the format:
  - As a [role], I want [goal], so that [benefit]
- Acceptance criteria are defined
- Scope is understood by the team
- Dependencies are identified
- Story is estimated
- No major unknowns remain

---

## 10. Tools & Collaboration Setup

- **Version Control:** GitHub
- **Project Management:** GitHub Projects (Kanban Board)
- **Communication:** Slack / Discord
- **Meetings:** Google Meet / Zoom
- **Documentation:** GitHub Wiki / Markdown files

---

## 11. Sprint Cadence

- Sprint length: 1–2 weeks
- Daily standup: 10–15 minutes
- Sprint planning: at the start of each sprint
- Sprint review/demo: end of sprint
- Retrospective: after each sprint

---

## 12. Team Success Criteria

The team is successful if:

- Sprint goals are consistently met
- Deliverables are completed on time
- The product meets stakeholder expectations
- The team collaborates effectively
- Risks and blockers are managed proactively

---

## 13. Commitment Statement

As the PolicyAI Squad, we commit to delivering a high-quality, trustworthy AI assistant by working collaboratively, following Agile principles, and maintaining a strong focus on accuracy, security, and user trust.
