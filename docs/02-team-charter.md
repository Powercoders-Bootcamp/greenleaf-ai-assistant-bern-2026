# 02. Team Charter

## Team Mission
Build a reliable AI assistant that gives accurate, source-based answers to common employee questions at GreenLeaf Logistics.

## Team Values
- Accuracy over speed
- Transparency over guessing
- Collaboration over isolation
- Respectful and proactive communication
- Shared ownership

## Team Roles
- Product Owner
- Scrum Master
- Tech Lead / Architect
- QA / Testing Lead

## Ground Rules
- Ask early when blocked
- Keep communication clear and short
- Document important decisions
- Respect deadlines and shared ownership
- No major technical decision without team alignment

## Definition of Done
A task is done when:
- it matches the agreed scope
- it is reviewed by the team
- it is documented
- it uses approved source data where relevant
- it does not expose sensitive information
