# GDPR / FADP Compliance Statement
### GreenLeaf AI Assistant ("BeatBot")

---

## 1. Scope and Purpose
[cite_start]This Statement outlines the technical and organizational measures implemented by **GreenLeaf** to ensure that the AI Assistant (**"BeatBot"**) complies with the **Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP)** and the **EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**[cite: 31, 61]. [cite_start]The system is engineered based on the principles of **Privacy by Design** and **Privacy by Default**, ensuring that data processing is restricted to the absolute minimum necessary for functional operation[cite: 32, 62].

## 2. Data Categories and Processing Activities
[cite_start]The system is designed to operate without the need for identifiable personal information[cite: 33, 63].

### Processed Data
* [cite_start]**User Input:** Free-text queries provided by the user[cite: 34, 64].
* [cite_start]**Technical Metadata:** Transient session identifiers and timestamps required for request routing[cite: 35, 65].

### Exclusions (Data Not Processed/Stored)
* [cite_start]**Personal Identifiers:** No names, email addresses, or employee IDs are linked to queries[cite: 36, 66].
* **Special Categories of Data:** The system does not solicit or process sensitive data as defined under Art. 9 GDPR or Art. [cite_start]5 nFADP (e.g., health, religious beliefs, or biometric data)[cite: 37, 38, 67, 68].
* [cite_start]**Persistent Tracking:** No IP profiling, MAC address logging, or cross-session tracking is performed[cite: 39, 69].

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing
Processing is conducted in strict adherence to the following legal frameworks:

| Framework | Legal Basis | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Swiss nFADP** | Proportionality & Legitimate Interest | [cite_start]To provide efficient, automated internal support (Art. 6 nFADP)[cite: 40, 70]. |
| **EU GDPR** | Art. 6(1)(f) (Legitimate Interest) | [cite_start]Enhancing internal operational efficiency[cite: 41, 71]. |
| **EU GDPR** | Art. 6(1)(b) (Contractual Necessity) | [cite_start]Providing tools essential for the fulfillment of employment-related inquiries[cite: 42, 72]. |

## 4. Purpose Limitation
[cite_start]Data processing is strictly confined to the following objectives[cite: 43, 73]:
1. [cite_start]Providing automated responses based on authorized internal documentation (e.g., Employee Handbooks)[cite: 43, 73].
2. [cite_start]Assisting in the interpretation of corporate policies (e.g., expense reports, leave entitlement)[cite: 44, 74].

> [cite_start]**Notice:** Data shall not be used for behavioral monitoring, automated performance evaluation, profiling (Art. 4(4) GDPR), or any third-party commercial purposes[cite: 45, 75].

## 5. Data Retention and Storage Architecture
[cite_start]The system utilizes a non-persistent architecture to ensure maximum privacy[cite: 46, 76].

* [cite_start]**In-Memory Processing:** User queries are processed in volatile memory and are purged immediately upon the delivery of the response[cite: 47, 77].
* [cite_start]**Persistent Storage:** By default, no database records of individual conversations are maintained[cite: 48, 78].

## 6. Information Security and Access Control
To prevent unauthorized data leakage and ensure the integrity of internal corporate information, BeatBot employs:

* **Input Validation:** Filtering mechanisms to detect and neutralize malicious code.
* **Refusal Logic:** Hard-coded restrictions prevent the disclosure of sensitive infrastructure data (e.g., Wi-Fi credentials).
* **Data Siloing:** The assistant cannot access or export data beyond its predefined, authorized knowledge base.

## 7. Data Subject Rights
In accordance with Chapter 4 of the nFADP and Articles 15-22 of the GDPR, users retain the right to information, access, and deletion.

* **Note on Applicability:** Since the system does not link queries to identifiable individuals, the ability to exercise these rights is limited.
* **Facilitation:** If anonymized logs are maintained, GreenLeaf will facilitate deletion requests where technically feasible.

## 8. International Data Transfers
All processing occurs within secured environments compliant with Swiss and EU standards. No data is transferred to third countries without an adequate level of protection as recognized by the Swiss Federal Council or the European Commission.

## 9. Conclusion
The GreenLeaf AI Assistant represents a compliant integration of AI technology within the workplace. By prioritizing **Data Minimization** and **Purpose Limitation**, the system ensures that employee privacy is upheld while providing high-utility automated support.
